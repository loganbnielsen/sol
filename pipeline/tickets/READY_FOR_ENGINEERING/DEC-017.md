**Depends on:** DEC-016 — the runner determines the surface. Revises the "Deploy & runtime visibility" paragraph in `docs/planning/ROADMAP.md`.

Decide where deploy/run diagnosis and runtime observability live, now that a deploy may be triggered by a push rather than by the user's terminal.

## Decision

**The surface follows the runner.** Whoever ran a deploy owns the record of it:

| Trigger | Runner | Where diagnosis lives |
|---|---|---|
| `sol up` / `sol deploy` locally | the user's machine | the CLI, run logs under `.sol/runs/` |
| their CI calls `sol deploy` | their CI | their CI logs/artifacts |
| push (hosted) | Sol's pipeline | the dashboard, a GitHub commit status, and a configured notification |
| push (self-hosted) | their Argo/CI | their cluster's tooling |

So **the CLI is a client of the platform for hosted deploys, not the owner of them**. For a push-triggered deploy the dashboard cannot be optional — the person who pushed is not at a terminal, and the commit status is what reaches them.

**Logs split by who generated them.** Deploy/run logs live *in the platform*, because the platform ran them: bounded, per-run, finite — an obligation that comes with running the deploy. Runtime logs stay *in the customer's cluster*: unbounded, retention-heavy, continuous — the September decision to avoid becoming a logging company stands. "It ran, so we hold the record" is not "we hold everything they ever emit".

**Diagnosis is factored once and rendered per surface.** One deploy-record + diagnosis model — `ImagePullBackOff` → the registry auth error, `OOMKilled` → the memory limit that was hit, unschedulable → no node with capacity — consumed by the CLI, the dashboard, and commit statuses. Built ad hoc inside `cmd_status.ml` it will be built three times, and the dashboard's version will be the good one while the CLI's drifts.

## Consequences

- The `docs/planning/ROADMAP.md` visibility paragraph is revised: its "`sol status` leads with Kubernetes-derived diagnosis, unconditionally" rule still holds for the local and GitOps modes; for hosted, the **dashboard** is the surface that leads.
- The four visibility items that section marks "not yet ticketed" (Kubernetes-derived diagnosis, `sol logs` runtime fallback, promtail scraping pod stdout, deploy run IDs) are re-scoped onto the factored model rather than implemented directly — and the run-ID/local-log item stays CLI-owned while the diagnosis rendering becomes shared.
- The CLI remains the right home for the *local* and *self-hosted* modes, which is most of Sol's usage today. This decision is about not building the hosted path's diagnosis into the CLI first.

## Acceptance criteria

- One diagnosis model exists and is rendered by at least two surfaces without duplicated interpretation logic.
- A push-triggered hosted deploy surfaces failure as a commit status plus a notification, not only in a dashboard.
- Deploy run records survive the cluster the run may have created or destroyed.
