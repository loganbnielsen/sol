Enforce subprocess deadlines until the child exits

**Depends on:** None.

**Premise verified (2026-09-28):** Read the implementation at `cli/lib/base/sol_cli_process.ml:105-156,224-237` and its representative callers/tests on origin/main `6a7b1fb5`. The described boundary remains present.

## Problem

The deadline is enforced only during pipe capture. After both output pipes close, blocking waitpid can exceed timeout_s indefinitely.

## Remediation

Keep the deadline active across both capture and child exit, then kill and reap a timed-out child through the shared runner.

## Acceptance criteria

- With timeout_s=0.05, both sleep 0.5 and exec 1>&- 2>&-; sleep 0.5 return Timeout promptly; successful short commands preserve their output/status.
- Update a runnable example/demo for application-facing behavior, or record why this is an internal-only refactor.
- Record the per-language capability verdict for framework/application contracts, or explain why language parity is unaffected.

## Completion (2026-09-29)

- **Premise re-verified** at origin/main `788e688d`: `Sol_cli_process.run` computed the deadline inside `capture_until_closed` and then called blocking `Unix.waitpid [] pid` unconditionally on the success path, so a child that had already closed both output pipes could outlive `timeout_s` (audit probe: `exec 1>&- 2>&-; sleep 0.5` returned `Ok` in 0.503 s at `timeout_s=0.05`).
- **Fix.** A `wait_for_exit` helper polls `waitpid WNOHANG` against the same absolute deadline used for capture (50 ms granularity, EINTR-safe), returning `Timed_out` when the deadline passes; the timed-out child is then killed and reaped by the shared `kill_and_reap`, so the `Timeout s` error is unchanged.
- **Tests** (bounded, `SIGALRM`-guarded): `sleep 0.5` and `exec 1>&- 2>&-; sleep 0.5` both return `Error (Timeout _)` and finish in under 0.4 s at `timeout_s=0.05`; `sh -c "echo ok"` at `timeout_s=5.0` still returns `Ok` with stdout `ok`. The closed-pipe case is the regression: it previously returned `Ok` after ~0.5 s.
- Validation: full `dune build`; `test_process.exe` 23/23 and `test_sol_process.exe` 23/23 pass; formatting clean.
- **Demo/example: not applicable** — CLI subprocess plumbing only. No language-parity impact: no framework or application contract changes.
