Release retry-topic records by due time instead of behind longer delays

**Depends on:** None.

**Premise verified (2026-09-29):** At `origin/main` `7f6aa377`, the relay handler (`framework/ocaml/kafka-eio-service/lib/kafka_service_retry_topics.ml:474-493`) processes one retry partition sequentially and sleeps until each record's `X-Sol-Retry-At` before touching the next. All attempts for a group share one retry topic (`relay_topic_name ... ~suffix:"retry"`). `kafka-eio-service.md:340-346` documents that "a retry delay blocks every later record sharing that retry partition" and calls the delay unbounded under backlog.

## Problem

Exponential backoff mixes short and long delays in one ordered log. A record on its 6th attempt, with a 600s delay, sits at the head of the retry partition. Every record behind it waits up to 600s, including first retries due 1s later, and new failures keep landing behind it. The effective retry delay is therefore the maximum outstanding delay, not the policy's delay. During an outage, when most retries happen, every record's retry is pushed toward `max_delay_s`, and records that would have succeeded quickly sit idle. The retry policy's schedule is not the schedule that runs.

The documentation records this as a limitation, but it is a design defect: the policy is part of the worker's contract, and the relay does not implement it.

## Remediation

Release each retry when it is due, independent of records with later due times. The conventional correct design is delay tiers: one retry topic per backoff tier, each holding only records of that one delay, so due order equals log order within a topic. The relay routes each retry to the tier matching its computed delay. It rounds up to the nearest tier and never schedules earlier than the policy says. Tier topics are provisioned and named with the same group scoping as today.

## Acceptance criteria

- In a broker-backed regression, a record with a 1s retry published after a record with a 60s retry is retried after about 1s, not about 60s.
- No retry runs earlier than its policy delay, and ordering remains documented as not guaranteed.
- Provisioning, naming (BUG-030 scoping) and DLQ routing cover every tier.
- `kafka-eio-service.md` drops the head-of-line caveat and states the actual schedule guarantee. Record the TypeScript retry parity verdict in completion notes.

## Completion notes

Closed as moot by FEAT-113 (2026-09-30). The defect was in
`kafka_service_retry_topics`'s relay, which FEAT-113 deleted along with
message-level retry itself (DEC-021's 2026-09-29 amendment: Kafka distributes
facts; jobs perform retryable work). There is no retry topic to release records
on, so the delay-inversion the ticket describes cannot occur. Work that still
needs delayed, independent delivery is a `sol-jobs` job with its own schedule.
TypeScript parity is tracked by FEAT-118.
