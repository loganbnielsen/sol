Refuse direct rotation of ExternalSecret-owned workload Secrets

**Depends on:** None.

**Premise verified (2026-09-29):** At `origin/main` `a99a09bd`, generated ExternalSecrets own `<service>-secrets` and refresh them. `Sol_cli_secret.list_workload_secrets` selects those targets by suffix, and `set`/`delete` mutate them without checking ownership. See the source trace and ESO contract in the audit report.

## Problem

In an External Secrets Operator deployment, `sol secret set` writes the target Kubernetes Secret directly and reports success after restarting workloads. ESO later reconciles the Secret from its external store, restoring the old value. The next restart can roll back credentials without another Sol command; `sol secret delete` has the same ownership conflict. This violates the reported rotation outcome and can cause an authentication outage when the operator revokes a value Sol claimed was installed.

## Remediation

Before the first mutation or restart, inspect all selected workload Secrets for an ExternalSecret owner or equivalent managed target. Refuse set/delete for that selection with an actionable instruction to rotate the external provider value and let ESO reconcile it. Keep direct rotation for Kubernetes-live Secrets. Do not rely solely on the caller's target flags: the live Secret's owner is the authority, and one operation may select several namespaces.

## Acceptance criteria

- A mixed selection containing an ExternalSecret-managed target fails before changing any Secret or restarting any workload; the error identifies the managed target and the provider-side rotation path.
- Kubernetes-live Secrets remain directly rotatable, and a later read confirms the new value survives without ESO ownership.
- Exercise set and delete with a stub or disposable ESO-backed cluster, including the mixed-namespace preflight case.
- Update the runnable GitOps tutorial's rotation guidance; completion notes record the cross-language verdict or explain why this CLI-only check has no application-language impact.

## Completion notes

**Premise re-verified 2026-10-01** at `origin/main @ 0d4fa913`. The premise was half
stale: `#737` (`BUG-093/refuse-eso-rotation`, "part A") had already landed the
remediation. `Sol_cli_secret.external_secret_targets` reads the live `ExternalSecret`
objects per namespace, and `refuse_external_secret_rotation` is called by both `set`
(`cli/lib/deploy/sol_cli_secret.ml:346`) and `delete` (`:379`) before any `apply`,
`patch` or `rollout restart`, over every selected namespace. That satisfies the first
two acceptance criteria and the remediation.

What part A did **not** deliver is criteria 3 and 4: nothing exercised the refusal, and
no rotation guidance described it. `rg -c 'externalsecret' cli/test/*.ml cli/test/*.sh`
matched only manifest-rendering tests, `rg -n 'external|eso|operator'
docs/deployment/credential-rotation.md` matched nothing, and `docs/guides/deployment.md`
sent readers to `sol secret set` with no ownership caveat. This change completes those
two criteria; the ticket moves to DONE.

**Added.**

- `cli/test/test_secret_rotation.ml` drives `Sol_cli_secret.set` and `delete` against a
  stub `kubectl` on `PATH` (the `test_deployment_state.ml` pattern) that serves a
  namespace-keyed manifest, logs every invocation, and reports "no matches for kind"

  which states the ownership conflict, the preflight refusal, and the provider-store

  separate step and points at it.
