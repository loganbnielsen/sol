## The problem

`Sol_cli_config` merges exactly two layers: `sol.yml` and the leaf `sol/<env>/<provider>/<region>.yml`. Environment-wide policy (`profile`, `scale`, `size`, alert routing, `base_domain`, `letsencrypt_email`, `node_failure_headroom_nodes`) has no layer of its own, so every region's file has to repeat it. Pluto's `prod` and `pilot` already repeat `base_domain`, `letsencrypt_email` and `node_failure_headroom_nodes`. A second region in an environment would copy the rest.

## Decision Required

1. Accept the three-layer model (`sol.yml` → environment → target, with every key overridable at a lower layer) or keep file-per-target.
2. File shape and name: a single `sol/environments.yml` keyed `<env>.targets.<provider>/<region>` (proposed), or another shape (proposal open question 4).
3. Which keys are allowed at environment level versus target level only. Proposed: identity keys (`cluster_name`, `kube_context`, `registry`, provider blocks) are target-only, and everything else may appear at either level.

The target address (`<env>/<provider>/<region>`, DEC-031's positional) does not change under any option.
