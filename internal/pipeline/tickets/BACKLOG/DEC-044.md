Decide whether source-topic decode failures may be acked and dropped when a DLQ exists

**Depends on:** None.

**Finding:** FND-0049 (`internal/pipeline/audits/findings/`).

**Premise verified 2026-09-23** against `origin/main @ f3e9480b` while filing (see the finding).

## Problem

`default_on_decode_error` acks and drops on the source topic even under `Retry_topics`, per the documented BUG-028 carve-out. A producer with an incompatible schema makes consumers ack through the whole topic; `Worker.Make*` exposes no override.

## Decision Required

Keep "never accepted, so skip is allowed" (status quo), or treat a decodable-failure on the source topic as failed work subject to the acknowledgement-ownership invariant when a DLQ is available?

## Remediation

If reversed: route source decode failures to the DLQ whenever one exists; make ack-and-drop an explicit opt-in on `Worker.Make*`; update `sol-worker.md`'s invariant text.

## Acceptance criteria

- Decision recorded; if reversed, an implementation ticket is filed.
