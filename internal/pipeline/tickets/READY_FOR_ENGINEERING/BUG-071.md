Preserve exclusive boundary ownership throughout deploy and rollback

**Depends on:** None.

**Premise verified (2026-09-28):** Traced `cli/bin/cmd_rollback.ml:101; cli/lib/deploy/sol_cli_boundary_lease.ml:30,78-109; cli/lib/deploy/sol_cli_deploy_run.ml:268-291` with callers and existing tests at origin/main `2dfa5694`. Evidence and reproduction limits are recorded in the source audit.

## Problem

Rollback discards its held lease and never heartbeats or verifies ownership. At 301 seconds its lease is reclaimable while it can still apply workloads, prune and move the release pointer. Deploy renews before each workload but does not maintain ownership through a long last apply and recording. Competing operations can therefore mutate the same boundary.

## Remediation

Maintain the existing lease through long-running operations and verify ownership before each subsequent mutation and pointer/record commit. Thread held ownership through rollback rather than discarding it. Raising the TTL alone is insufficient. Document the fencing/stop behavior for an operation already in progress when ownership is lost.

## Acceptance criteria

- A controllable-clock/takeover regression shows the old owner cannot perform further apply, prune, pointer or release-record mutations after losing ownership. Long healthy rollback/deploy renews its lease; release remains ownership-checked. Update a runnable rollback example or explicitly explain why its existing example remains applicable.
- Record the application demo and per-language verdict in completion notes wherever the change affects an application contract.
