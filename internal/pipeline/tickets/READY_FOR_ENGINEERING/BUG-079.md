Enforce not-before claims for verified JWT authentication

**Depends on:** None.

**Premise verified (2026-09-28):** Traced `framework/ocaml/sol-svc/lib/auth_internal.ml:213,295-315` and representative callers/tests at origin/main `6eb36b2c`. The audit records reproduced behavior, positive controls and practical limits.

## Problem

An authentically signed HS256 token with valid issuer/audience/expiration and nbf one hour in the future authenticates immediately. The selected Jose verifier checks expiration but does not enforce nbf. This permits access before the issuer's declared validity window. Fractional expiration additionally raises Type_error and becomes HTTP 500; it does not bypass expiration.

## Remediation

Validate supported temporal claims at the verified authentication boundary, enforcing present nbf and expiration semantics with finite NumericDate handling. Preserve signature verification, issuer/audience checks, and existing optional-claim policy. Convert malformed claims into typed authorization failures rather than exception-driven 500 responses. Record any intentional clock-skew policy explicitly.

## Acceptance criteria

- Signed future-nbf tokens are rejected; valid/current tokens authenticate; expired and malformed temporal claims fail safely. Include integer/fractional NumericDate boundary cases and tests through the HTTP auth adapter. Update the runnable auth example and explicitly record the TypeScript capability verdict.
- Completion notes identify runnable example/demo coverage and the per-language capability verdict, or explain why either is not applicable.
