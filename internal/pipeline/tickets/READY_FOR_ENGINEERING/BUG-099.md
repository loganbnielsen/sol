Make Kafka worker readiness hold for replicas that own no partition

**Depends on:** None.

**Premise verified (2026-09-29):** At `origin/main` `7f6aa377`:

- `framework/ocaml/kafka-eio-service/lib/kafka_service_config.ml:55` fixes `partitions = 1` for every topic Sol creates.
- `framework/ocaml/sol-worker/lib/worker_health.ml` reports `/readyz` 200 only while partitions are assigned.
- `cli/lib/workspace/sol_cli_manifest_yaml.ml:288-293` renders both the worker `startupProbe` (`failureThreshold 60 × periodSeconds 5`) and its `readinessProbe` on `/readyz`.
- `cli/lib/deploy/sol_cli_deployment_plan.ml:571-579` requires `replicas >= 2` for a `node-failure-tolerant` worker, and `Sol_cli_deployment_render` adds a PDB with `minAvailable = replicas - 1`.

## Problem

With one partition, only one member of a consumer group can own it. Every other replica is idle by design, but Sol reports it as not ready, and its startup probe never passes. As a result:

- **Idle replicas crash-loop.** After 300s of unready `/readyz`, the kubelet restarts the standby container, and restarts back off exponentially. The replica that should take over after a node loss is often in `CrashLoopBackOff` when the node goes. `node-failure-tolerant` is the profile this breaks, and Sol requires it to have at least two replicas.
- **Node drains block.** The PDB counts only the owning replica as healthy (1 of `minAvailable` 1), so evicting that replica is never allowed. `kubectl drain` of its node hangs.
- **Rollouts can stall.** Under the default `RollingUpdate` (`maxUnavailable` rounds to 0 below four replicas), a new pod becomes ready only if the group rebalance happens to give it the partition. If it doesn't, the old pod keeps it, the new pod's startup probe kills it at 300s, and the rollout makes no progress.

Readiness here conflates "healthy and in the group" with "currently owns work". The hard-coded single partition also means extra replicas can never add throughput.

**Reproduction** (local Redpanda, two `Worker.Make_with_retry` processes with one `group_id`, each serving `/readyz` on its own port): polled at 10s, 20s and 30s, replica 1 returned `200` and replica 2 returned `503` every time. `rpk` showed one partition, owned by a single member.

## Remediation

Readiness for a consumer worker should mean the process is healthy and a member of its group, with liveness still tracking poll cadence (BUG-014). Assignment of work is a separate condition. Do not use an assignment-gated endpoint as a startup probe. Separately, make partition count part of the declared topic contract, so a worker's replica count and its source topic's partitions are validated together. For example, refuse, or warn at plan time, when `replicas` exceeds the partitions of the topic it consumes.

## Acceptance criteria

- Two replicas of one worker on a one-partition topic both pass their startup and readiness probes, and neither restarts over a 10-minute window.
- `kubectl drain` of the node running the owning replica succeeds under the rendered PDB.
- A `replicas = 1` rolling update completes whichever member the rebalance assigns.
- Partition count is declared or derived, and the plan refuses or flags a worker whose replicas exceed its source partitions.
- `docs/deployment/workload-availability.md` states the new readiness meaning. Update a runnable example and record the TypeScript worker parity verdict in completion notes.
