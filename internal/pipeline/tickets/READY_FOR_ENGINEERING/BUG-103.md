Renew sol-jobs leases while a handler runs so claims stay exclusive

**Depends on:** None.

**Premise verified (2026-09-29):** At `origin/main` `7f6aa377`, `framework/ocaml/sol-jobs/sol-jobs.md:105-110` states that leases are not renewed, so "a handler that outlives `lease_s` is not exclusive". It leaves renewal out of scope and asks apps to "size `lease_s` for the slowest handler". `sol_jobs.ml` sets `locked_until` once, in `claim_q`, and never extends it.

## Problem

A job queue's core guarantee is that one worker runs a claimed job at a time. `sol-jobs` gives that up for any handler slower than a static number chosen in advance:

- **Overrunning handlers run concurrently.** Once `lease_s` passes, another poller claims the job and runs it while the first run is still executing. Fenced finalize (BUG-050) keeps the *queue row* consistent, but it cannot undo two concurrent executions of the side effect: two emails, two charges, two exports.
- **The escape hatch has its own cost.** Raising `lease_s` to cover the slowest handler makes crash recovery that much slower for every job, since a dead worker's job waits the full lease.

Those are opposite requirements on one knob, and the "lease overrun" warning only reports the violation after it has happened. Idempotent handlers are still needed for crash redelivery. But exclusivity while the claimant is alive is a separate property the queue should provide.

## Remediation

While `J.handle` runs, a sibling fiber extends `locked_until` with the same fenced `id = ? AND attempts = ?` predicate, at an interval well inside the lease. If a renewal matches no row, the lease has been lost: stop renewing, log it, and make the finalize fenced as today. Keep the lease short, so crash recovery stays fast. Remove "size `lease_s` for the slowest handler" from the contract.

## Acceptance criteria

- In a Postgres-backed regression, a handler running for 5× `lease_s` is never claimed by a second poller, and its finalize succeeds.
- A killed worker's job is reclaimed within about one short lease, independent of handler duration.
- A renewal that fails because the row was re-claimed or deleted stops renewing and is logged. The stale holder's finalize stays fenced.
- `sol-jobs.md` states the exclusivity guarantee. Record the TypeScript jobs parity verdict in completion notes.
