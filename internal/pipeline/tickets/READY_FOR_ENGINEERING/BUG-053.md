`sol-svc`: make the JWKS refresh single-flight and Eio-safe, and turn every exception outside the handler into a counted 500

**Depends on:** None.

**Finding:** FND-0050 (`internal/pipeline/audits/findings/`).

**Premise verified 2026-09-24** against `origin/main @ fd5c7e0c`, behaviourally: two
concurrent validations with a yielding fetch give
`Sys_error("Mutex.lock: Resource deadlock avoided")` on the second, and cohttp-eio
answers a raising callback with an empty reply.

## Problem

`get_jwks` (`auth_internal.ml:182-198`) holds a `Stdlib.Mutex` across the HTTPS fetch.
A second request fiber that misses the cache while the first is fetching raises
`Sys_error` from `Mutex.protect`. `dispatch` runs authentication outside the handler's
`try` (`service.ml:145-173`), so the exception reaches cohttp-eio. The client gets
no HTTP response, and no metric is recorded. This happens at startup and every 300 s
under concurrent load, for every `Jwks_url` service. Separately, a token signed with a
newly rotated key (unknown `kid`) is rejected until the TTL expires.

## Remediation

1. Replace the `Stdlib.Mutex` in `get_jwks` with a single-flight refresh that
   concurrent misses await: an `Eio.Mutex`, or a shared `Eio.Promise` for the refresh
   in flight. Keep `validate` Eio-agnostic apart from what the injected fetch needs.
2. On an unknown `kid` with a fresh cache, refetch once, rate-limited (at most once
   per 30 s), before rejecting.
3. Widen `dispatch`'s boundary: any exception other than cancellation and fatal ones,
   raised during authentication, body reading or the handler, becomes
   `Response.internal_error` and is logged, so the request gets a 500 and is counted.

## Acceptance criteria

- Test: two concurrent validations against a fetch that yields both succeed, with
  exactly one fetch.
- Test: an unknown `kid` triggers one refetch and succeeds when the new JWKS has it.
- Test: an exception raised in authentication yields a 500 response through `dispatch`.
- Mutation check: restoring `Stdlib.Mutex` fails the concurrency test.
- Demo/example: state applicability in completion notes.
- TS parity: record the `@sol-fab` JWKS verdict (DEC-022).
