Route source-topic decode failures to the DLQ when one exists; make ack-and-drop an explicit opt-in

**Depends on:** None.

**Finding:** FND-0049 (`internal/pipeline/audits/findings/`).

**Decision:** recorded as step 6 ("Runtime track (G)") of `internal/pipeline/audits/HARDEN-004-handoff.md`: *"source-topic decode failures go to the DLQ where one exists, ack-and-drop only as explicit opt-in."* That supersedes the BUG-028 non-goal carve-out (`sol-worker.md:258`).

**Premise verified 2026-09-23** against `origin/main @ f3e9480b`: `default_on_decode_error` (`kafka_service.ml:306-310`) acks and drops, and `Kafka_service_retry_topics.consume` passes source decode errors to it even though `dlq_topic_name` exists.

## Problem

Under `Retry_topics`, a source-topic record that fails to decode is acked and dropped although a group-scoped DLQ is provisioned. A producer deployed with an incompatible schema makes every consumer ack through the topic; the only trace is `sol_worker_decode_errors_total`. `Worker.Make*` offers no override.

## Remediation

In `Retry_topics` mode, route source decode failures to the DLQ with the `X-Sol-Decode-Error` diagnostic header (reuse `route_retry_decode_error`'s relay shape), acking only after the publish succeeds. Expose the decode policy on `Worker.Make*` (`Dead_letter` default when a DLQ exists; `Skip` as explicit opt-in). Under `In_memory` (no DLQ), keep skip-and-ack but make it explicit in the API and docs. Update the acknowledgement-ownership invariant in `sol-worker.md`.

## Acceptance criteria

- Test: under `Retry_topics`, an undecodable source record is published to the DLQ with raw payload, key, headers and the decode diagnostic, and acked only after the publish succeeds; a publish failure leaves it unacked.
- Test: the explicit skip opt-in still acks and drops.
- `sol-worker.md` / `kafka-eio-service.md` invariant text updated.
- TS parity: record the `@sol-fab/worker` verdict or a tracking ticket (DEC-022).
- Demo/example: the retry demo shows the DLQ path, or completion notes say why not.
