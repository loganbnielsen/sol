**Depends on:** None.

## Problem

`@sol-fab/kafka@0.5.1` declares `"@sol-fab/obs": "^0.1.0"`, which resolves to
`0.1.2`. `@sol-fab/obs` moved to `0.4.0` with `OBS-051` (the injection-aware
workload-identity reader). Any consumer that depends on both — and
`examples/pluto/app/demo_ts` does, after `OBS-051` — resolves **two** copies: its
own `0.4.0` for `makeLokiPusher`/`resourceAttributes`, and `0.1.2` for the
`traceparentOf`/`extractTraceparent` that `@sol-fab/kafka` re-exports.

Reproduced on `main` before the fix:

```console
$ cd examples/pluto/app/demo_ts
$ npm ls @sol-fab/obs
demo-ts@ /home/lbendtly/Code/sol/examples/pluto/app/demo_ts
├─┬ @demo-ts/contract@ -> ./contract
│ └─┬ @sol-fab/kafka@0.5.1
│   └── @sol-fab/obs@0.1.2
├─┬ fulfillment-worker@ -> ./fulfillment_worker
│ └── @sol-fab/obs@0.4.0
└─┬ order-svc@ -> ./order_svc
  └── @sol-fab/obs@0.4.0
```

Nothing misbehaves today: `traceparentOf`/`extractTraceparent` are unchanged
between `0.1.2` and `0.4.0`. But this is a second copy of a package whose whole
purpose is to be the single shared vocabulary (DEC-022), and the next `obs` move
would leave an app's re-exported tracing and its own `@sol-fab/obs` as two
implementations of one contract — silently, because both compile.

## Remediation

- [ ] `@sol-fab/kafka` widens its dependency to `@sol-fab/obs@^0.4.0` and
      releases `0.5.2` through the tag-driven OIDC/`provenance` workflow.
- [ ] The FEAT-084 TypeScript templates (`platform/shared/templates/{svc,worker}-ts`)
      move from `@sol-fab/obs@^0.3.0` to `^0.4.0`, so a scaffolded unit is
      identity-aware instead of pinned below the identity reader.
- [ ] `examples/pluto/app/demo_ts` refreshes its lockfile and resolves a single
      `@sol-fab/obs@0.4.0`.
- [ ] `internal/ci/check_platform_component_drift.py` asserts the demo lockfile
      resolves exactly one `@sol-fab/obs` at or above the identity floor, and
      applies its obs-floor check to the template packages as well as the demo.

## Acceptance criteria

- `npm ls @sol-fab/obs` in `examples/pluto/app/demo_ts` reports exactly one
  version, `>= 0.4.0`.
- The TypeScript golden-path smoke still passes, so the `OBS-051` log/trace
  identity behavior is intact.
- The guard fails when a second `@sol-fab/obs` version reappears in the lockfile
  or when a template falls below the identity floor.
